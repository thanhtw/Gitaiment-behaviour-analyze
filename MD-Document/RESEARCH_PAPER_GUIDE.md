Current facts:

- Version-1 and Version-2 are combined before analysis.

- The per-user table contains 105 distinct usernames.
- K-means uses 11 behavioral/derived inputs; outcomes are withheld for profiling.
- Gap Statistic selects four clusters with sizes 21, 44, 31, and 9.
- Alternative validity indices disagree, and this sensitivity must be disclosed.
- Sequence and transition findings are associations, not causal or predictive evidence.
- SPSS is not part of the current workflow.
